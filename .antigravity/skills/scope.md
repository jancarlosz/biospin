# Contracted Scope

## 1. Project

Project: New institutional website for BioSpin.

Contracted plan: Complete Plan.

The scope is defined by:

* `docs/PROJECT-SPEC.md`
* `docs/PROPOSAL.md`

This document summarizes scope boundaries and must be used to prevent uncontrolled expansion.

---

## 2. Included — Public Website

The project includes:

* Home;
* About BioSpin;
* Solutions hub;
* Nanofiberdressing page;
* OncoMatrix page;
* Blog / News;
* Individual blog posts;
* Contact page.

The website includes responsive design and institutional content presentation.

---

## 3. Included — Solutions

The project includes:

* solution catalog;
* individual solution pages;
* solution information;
* SEO metadata where applicable;
* solution-specific lead forms when defined in the client scope;
* management of catalog content through the administrative panel.

The catalog is a digital showcase.

It is not an e-commerce system.

---

## 4. Included — Blog

The project includes:

* post creation;
* post editing;
* post deletion;
* category management;
* image uploads;
* draft status;
* publication status;
* public post listing;
* individual post pages;
* individual SEO fields.

The project does not include recurring content production.

---

## 5. Included — Administrative Panel

The administrative panel includes:

* protected login;
* post management;
* category management;
* solution catalog management;
* publication control;
* image handling required for managed content.

The panel is not a generic CMS or visual page builder.

---

## 6. Included — Lead Capture

The project includes:

* contact forms;
* solution-specific forms where defined;
* Resend integration;
* validation;
* basic anti-spam protection;
* lead origin identification.

The origin may identify pages or solutions such as:

* contact;
* home;
* Nanofiberdressing;
* OncoMatrix.

The proposal requires lead identification and email delivery.

A complete CRM is not included.

---

## 7. Included — Technical Delivery

The project includes:

* technical SEO setup;
* titles and descriptions;
* friendly URLs;
* sitemap;
* robots.txt;
* image optimization;
* semantic structure;
* social sharing metadata;
* performance best practices;
* security best practices;
* GitHub versioning;
* initial deployment.

The project includes technical SEO preparation.

It does not include:

* monthly SEO management;
* recurring content production;
* guaranteed Google rankings.

---

## 8. Explicitly Excluded

Unless separately approved, do not implement:

* shopping cart;
* online payments;
* inventory management;
* order management;
* full CRM;
* sales pipeline;
* lead assignment;
* advanced analytics dashboard;
* visual page builder;
* advanced multi-role permission system;
* mobile application;
* recurring content registration service;
* recurring SEO management;
* ranking guarantees;
* pages outside the agreed structure;
* third-party integrations outside the proposal;
* complex automation workflows.

---

## 9. Cosmetic Line Ambiguity

The client scope references a cosmetic line as part of the BioSpin portfolio.

However, the detailed page architecture currently specifies individual product pages primarily for:

* Nanofiberdressing;
* OncoMatrix.

Do not automatically create a detailed cosmetic solution page unless:

* the client provides the content; and
* the requirement is confirmed.

If necessary, treat this as an ambiguity requiring confirmation rather than inventing a page.

---

## 10. Leads

The contract includes:

* form capture;
* email delivery via Resend;
* page or solution origin identification.

Do not automatically add:

* CRM stages;
* lead dashboards;
* sales workflows;
* assignment;
* follow-up automation;
* lead analytics.

These may be suggested as future improvements but are not automatically included.

---

## 11. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing required project materials, including:

* logo;
* visual identity;
* institutional texts;
* solution information;
* images;
* contact information;
* social links;
* domain access;
* hosting access when applicable;
* email recipient for leads;
* necessary integration access.

Do not invent missing institutional or scientific information.

---

## 12. Change of Scope Rule

A request may represent a scope change when it requires:

* new pages;
* new integrations;
* new user flows;
* new database domains;
* significant architecture changes;
* functionality not described in the proposal.

When this happens:

1. Identify the request as a potential scope change.
2. Explain what is required.
3. Identify affected areas.
4. Do not silently implement major new functionality.

---

## 13. Final Scope Principle

The goal is to deliver everything that was contracted.

Do not under-deliver.

Do not silently over-deliver complex features that create additional development, maintenance or support obligations.

When there is ambiguity, prefer clarification or the smallest implementation that clearly fulfills the agreed requirement.
